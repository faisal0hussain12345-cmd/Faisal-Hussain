## Rights and publication check

The Department of Energy gallery identifies the subjects and historical captions, but this project does not assume that every image is automatically public domain. The U.S. National Park Service also warns that copyright status may vary and asks users to check individual image detail pages. Confirm item-level rights and any required credit/permissions before publishing or monetizing the finished video.

Additional primary-source image and film collections:
- DOE Manhattan Project historical resources: https://www.energy.gov/lm/manhattan-project-historical-resources
- National Park Service photos and multimedia: https://www.nps.gov/mapr/learn/photosmultimedia/index.htm
- National Archives guide to Office of the Chief of Engineers records, including Trinity-test motion pictures and Hiroshima/Nagasaki photographs: https://www.archives.gov/research/guide-fed-records/groups/077.html

The current automated render uses the three DOE image references above when downloads succeed. Other scenes still use designed evidence-board graphics; no unverified image is represented as an original historical photograph.
